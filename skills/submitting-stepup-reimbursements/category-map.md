## Likely ineligible (Appendix A, short list, for information only)
Household items not on the classroom list, medical items, items failing the age rule, arcade or theme-park extras, tuition extras, live animals, motorized vehicles, gaming subscriptions, cash purchases from private sellers, anything paid by another source.

## Rule: pick the best category, do not judge eligibility
Step Up decides eligibility, not the skill. For every receipt the user asks to submit, choose the closest Category > Type > Description from the table above and submit it. If the item fits nothing well, use Instructional Material > School Supplies or the nearest Type, and write the item name plainly in the learning text. Mention in the final report when an item is not named in the guide, so the user knows a denial is possible. Never refuse or hold a receipt for that reason once the user said to submit.

## Portal quirk: slow document read resets the form
After upload the portal may "read" the document for up to 60+ seconds, then show "Oops! Something went wrong". If you click Continue to details before it finishes, the late result can wipe the form. Wait for the Oops or results screen before continuing. Always check the Summary shows the item and the right total. If it shows $0.00, go back, re-enter the item, and do not submit.

## Portal quirk: dropdowns
Long dropdowns (Type, Description, Vendor) can open below the viewport. Scroll the page so the list is visible, scroll inside the list if needed, click the option by coordinates, and take a screenshot to confirm the field changed. `scroll_to` + ref click works for Category.
